# Scout Maintenance Guide

The application is substantially built. The Scouts' role is to keep it accurate,
traceable and useful as Town projects change.

## Scout team roles

### 1. Source finder

- Check the Town Board agenda and minutes.
- Check the Town bid and document pages.
- Save the direct link to every new document.
- Record the date the document was posted.

### 2. Data verifier

- Match every amount to the source.
- Confirm whether the amount is estimated, proposed, approved, bid, awarded or spent.
- Confirm the source page number.
- Flag conflicting numbers instead of choosing one silently.

### 3. Project updater

- Update the timeline and current status.
- Add newly verified bids, awards, funding and photographs.
- Preserve the earlier history when the project moves to a new stage.

### 4. Quality checker

- Open every link.
- Test the application on a computer and phone.
- Recalculate one benchmark manually.
- Check spelling, dates, captions and source labels.

## Fall update workflow

1. Select one project.
2. Look for new Town records.
3. Complete `SOURCE_VERIFICATION_CHECKLIST.md`.
4. Update `data/projects.js`.
5. Add new Town-owned images or documents under `assets/`.
6. Preview the site and test the calculator.
7. Ask an adult project lead to review the change.
8. Commit the verified update to GitHub.

## Rules

- Do not invent missing information.
- Do not use a newspaper value as a Town-verified final cost.
- Do not call a bid an award without the Board resolution or award notice.
- Do not show a tax impact when the Town has not supplied enough information.
- Do not remove caution notes merely to make a project look more complete.
