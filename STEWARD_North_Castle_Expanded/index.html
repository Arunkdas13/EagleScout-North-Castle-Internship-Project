What is STEWARD?

One understandable path from a Town proposal to a resident’s bottom line.

STEWARD stands for Simple Transparency & Education With Accessible Resident Data. It turns scattered public records into a consistent, source-linked explanation of each capital project.

01

What is proposed?

Purpose, location, scope and the public need.

02

Where is it now?

Review, approval, design, bidding, construction or completion.

03

What does it cost?

Estimates, bids, awards and actual spending stay clearly labeled.

04

How is it funded?

Fund balance, bonds, grants, contributions and other sources.

05

How could it affect me?

Resident impact is calculated only when a documented method exists.

      The STEWARD standard

      Facts, estimates and unknowns stay separate.

      1

      Collect

      Gather Town PDFs, bid files, Board materials, financial models and verified staff responses.

      2

      Classify

      Label every figure as estimated, proposed, approved, awarded, spent or awaiting verification.

      3

      Calculate

      Only calculate household impact when authoritative inputs and a defensible formula are available.

      4

      Maintain

      Scouts re-check sources, add new records and update projects as they move forward.

      The Eagle Scout project continues

      Scouts will keep STEWARD accurate, current and accessible.

      The application is the foundation. The continuing service is maintaining its public value as projects, bids, funding decisions and resident needs change.

      01

      Verify the data

      Trace every figure to a Town record or named official, test links and flag estimates or missing information.

      02

      Update and adapt

      Add new bids, approvals, funding decisions and completed-project figures; adapt the format as new fall projects emerge.

      03

      Teach residents

      Offer in-person demonstrations and simple online guidance, especially for older residents and people who are less comfortable with technology.

      04

      Build civic confidence

      Help residents use the application, open the original sources and understand how local decisions connect to public finances.
